2026-10-03 · Day 7Delegation｜授权
As a manager, I don't need to do everything myself. My job is to set clear goals, define the standard, and make sure everyone knows what they are responsible for. I can give my team more freedom, but I still need to follow up on key milestones. Good delegation is not about losing control. It is about helping the team take ownership and deliver better results.
作为管理者，我不需要所有事情都自己做。我的工作是设定清晰目标、明确标准，并确保每个人都知道自己负责什么。我可以给团队更多自主空间，但仍需要跟进关键节点。好的授权不是失去控制，而是让团队真正承担责任，并交付更好的结果。

2026-10-04 · Day 8Feedback｜有效反馈
When I give feedback, I try to be specific. Instead of saying, “This is not good enough,” I explain what the problem is and what needs to change. I also try to separate the person from the problem. The goal is not to criticize someone, but to help them improve. Clear feedback helps the team understand the standard and make better decisions next time.
当我给反馈时，我会尽量具体。与其说“这个做得不够好”，我会解释问题到底在哪里，以及需要改变什么。我也会尽量把“人”和“问题”分开。反馈的目的不是批评一个人，而是帮助他进步。清晰的反馈能帮助团队理解标准，并在下一次做出更好的判断。

2026-10-05 · Day 9Prioritization｜优先级
When everything feels important, we need to set priorities. I usually ask three questions: What has the biggest impact on the result? What needs to be done now? And what can wait? A good manager does not try to do everything at the same time. The key is to focus on the most important problem and use our time and resources effectively.
当所有事情看起来都很重要时，我们需要确定优先级。我通常会问三个问题：什么对结果影响最大？什么必须现在做？什么可以等等？一个好的管理者不会试图同时做完所有事情。关键是聚焦最重要的问题，并有效利用时间和资源。

2026-10-06 · Day 10Data-driven Decision Making｜数据判断
The data tells us what happened, but it does not always tell us why. When sales go down, we should not jump to conclusions. We need to break down the data, compare different factors, and find the real cause. A good decision is based on evidence, not just experience or intuition.
数据告诉我们发生了什么，但不一定告诉我们为什么。当销售下降时，我们不应该急着下结论。我们需要拆解数据、比较不同因素，并找到真正的原因。一个好的决策应该基于证据，而不只是经验或直觉。

2026-10-14 · Day 18Customer Retention｜客户留存
Getting a new customer is important, but keeping an existing customer is just as important. A successful business does not only focus on the first purchase. It also creates reasons for customers to come back. To improve customer retention, we need to deliver a good product experience, build trust, and understand what customers need after their first purchase. Long-term growth comes from both acquiring new customers and retaining existing ones.
获得新客户很重要，但留住现有客户同样重要。成功的企业不仅关注客户的第一次购买，还会创造让客户再次购买的理由。为了提升客户留存，我们需要提供良好的产品体验、建立信任，并理解客户首次购买后的需求。长期增长既来自新客户的获取，也来自现有客户的留存。
